29 October 2026, PR #91. This assessment uses actual processing evidence, the owner's confirmation that **Google Workspace Business Starter is active for Colorful Life Ltd**, standard service terms and the official Cloudinary DPF record. It supersedes the earlier blanket requests for account-console exports and individually verified acceptance records. No terms were accepted or provider settings changed by this review.

Linking their notices does not transfer our controller responsibility. This review does not audit provider infrastructure or duplicate provider compliance programmes.
