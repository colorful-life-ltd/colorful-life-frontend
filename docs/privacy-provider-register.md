## Other flows and limits

No standalone analytics/advertising integration was identified in the reviewed frontend source. This is not a network/cookie audit of payment SDKs. GitHub Actions is used for source/build/deployment, not a configured customer database upload; never put customer exports or unredacted logs in artifacts. Cloudflare Turnstile is an optional backend checkout-challenge capability; before enabling it, add its actual activation, browser/security-data flow and terms to this register. No account-specific enablement was verified here. Customer information sent to advisers/accountants or stored in spreadsheets also requires owner inventory.

For each PENDING record the owner can store a private, minimal evidence note: legal entity/account alias, agreement/version/date, role and service, transfer initiator/recipient, mechanism, current certification evidence if used, any required assessment, subprocessor review and next review date. Do not commit contracts containing personal account information or credentials. Adequacy and provider-initiated onward transfers should be distinguished from transfers the company initiates; do not demand or claim a TRA indiscriminately. A published DPA alone is not proof of this account's coverage.

**Completion rule:** no new legal claim or removal of transferSafeguards until active flows have an evidenced applicable arrangement or are confirmed disabled. ICO formal reference pending is not itself a blocker. This register documents remaining work; it does not certify the shop as production-ready.
