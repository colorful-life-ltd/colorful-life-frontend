### Support-mail retention and copies

Quarterly manual review covers support mail/attachments, exports/downloads, snapshots and outstanding retention cases. **PROPOSED, NOT OWNER-APPROVED:** review routine resolved support correspondence 12 months after resolution/last substantive contact; review is not automatic deletion or a guaranteed disposal deadline. Necessary transaction, dispute and rights-request evidence follows its justified schedule/hold, not a blanket mailbox purge. Confirm actual mailbox settings, Trash/Vault/hold behaviour, forwarding destinations and independent copies before claiming disposal. Workspace billing or published deletion terms do not implement our review process. See [retention operations](retention-operations.md).

## Remaining transferSafeguards evidence and completion rule

1. Owner confirms the active production **and testing** inventory, including inbound mail/forwarding, actual carriers/advisers, optional lookup/challenge services and any exports. Unknown activation remains pending, not excluded.
2. For each active flow, retain a private minimal record of account alias/legal entities, service/role, agreement/version/effective applicability, recipients/territories, subprocessor review and relevant UK transfer mechanism. Record any required assessment and supplementary measures. A public DPA, welcome email or configuration setting alone cannot close this item.
3. For adequacy/UK Extension reliance, verify current recipient certification and data/service scope; distinguish provider-initiated onward transfers from transfers the company initiates. Do not demand a transfer assessment indiscriminately for every adequate destination, or infer adequacy from London hosting.
4. Reconcile the public Privacy Policy with those verified facts and obtain owner approval of the resulting wording. Remove a gate only after underlying evidence is reviewed; this register does not certify readiness. ICO reference pending alone is not a blocker.

No standalone analytics/advertising integration was identified in source; this is not a cookie/network audit of payment SDKs. GitHub Actions handles source/build/deployment, not a configured customer database export; account/developer metadata is separate from storefront customer processing. Do not place private contracts, credentials, mailbox bodies or customer exports in Git/artifacts.
